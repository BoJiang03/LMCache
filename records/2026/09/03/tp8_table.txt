CORRECTED by records/2026/09/03/6_..., 10:23.  Every ms/step in this file
derived from the MEDIAN of Avg prompt throughput is a classifier, not a
measurement: the log line has two quantised attractors (~95,99x and
~89,99x) and the median only reports which one an arm sits on.  By step
probe (differenced), the same arms are:

  tp8_none 83.94   tp8_nostore 85.71 (+1.77)   tp8_tinykey 88.53 (+4.59)
  tp8_mp 91.90 (+7.97)
  tp8sp_none 84.10  tp8sp_mp 91.38 (+7.28)   <- pool 1,920,000
  c1000_none 132.01 c1000_mp 133.11 (+1.10)   <- TP=4, pool 1,920,000

Each delta x 7200 steps matches that arm end-to-end delta within ~1 s.
So: pool depth is NOT irrelevant to four significant figures (7.28 vs
7.97), and TP=4 does NOT show zero steady-state loss (+1.10, 0.83%).
The conclusions survive; the precision claims do not.
